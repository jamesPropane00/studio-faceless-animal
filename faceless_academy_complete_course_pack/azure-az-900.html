1. A team must choose the BEST first step in Cloud concepts. What is generally strongest?

Good professional practice begins by understanding requirements, risk, and accountability.

2. Which choice most closely reflects mature practice in Azure architecture and services?

Mature practice is repeatable, documented, measurable, and risk-based.

3. When two controls address the same risk, what should drive selection?

Control selection should be based on requirements and risk treatment.

4. What does least privilege mean?

Least privilege reduces unnecessary access.

5. Which evidence is strongest?

Professional conclusions require dependable evidence.

6. What should happen after a meaningful change?

Verification confirms that the change achieved its goal without unacceptable side effects.
