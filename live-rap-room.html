TikTok Gaming LIVE setup
  1. Press Release Mic to TikTok before starting LIVE.
  2. In TikTok, start Gaming LIVE and choose screen sharing.
  3. Let TikTok use the microphone and enable Device/Media audio.
  4. This page supplies Station One, beats, videos and pads; TikTok alone captures your voice.
The browser microphone must remain fully released during TikTok LIVE. Camera-only controls are safe. Browser voice effects and Hear My Mic cannot run in this mode because they would reopen a second microphone.
