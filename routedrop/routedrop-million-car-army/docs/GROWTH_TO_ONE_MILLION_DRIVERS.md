# Growth Plan: From 0 to a Million Registered Cars

A million-car network is a long-term outcome, not a launch claim. The strategy is corridor-by-corridor liquidity.

## Growth equation

Supply grows when:

`expected runner earnings per added minute × job frequency × trust > signup friction`

Demand grows when:

`price advantage + convenience + reliability > existing alternatives`

## Stage 0 — Proof (0–100 runners)

One dense corridor. Recruit recurring commuters, gig workers, students, security workers, and local resellers. Build 5–10 anchor businesses before public marketing.

Targets:
- 100 registered runners
- 25 weekly active runners
- 10 anchor businesses
- 100 completed jobs
- <5% serious support incidents

## Stage 1 — Metro liquidity (100–10,000)

Clone the corridor playbook within one metro. Every job creates:

- Recipient invitation
- Runner referral link
- Business referral credit
- Shareable earnings/impact card
- Saved recurring route

Do not open a ZIP code until minimum supply/demand thresholds are met. Maintain a waitlist and show realistic service windows.

## Stage 2 — Multi-market operating system (10,000–100,000)

- Market launch dashboard
- Local ambassador program
- University/commuter partnerships
- Reseller and repair-shop verticals
- Business bulk upload/API
- Automated trust/risk system
- Market-level pricing and incentive controls

## Stage 3 — National network (100,000–1,000,000 registered cars)

Expand only through approved legal/insurance templates. The national advantage comes from recurring routes and interoperable trust profiles, not instant availability everywhere.

Potential national channels:

- Marketplace sellers and buyers
- Retail returns and store transfers
- Auto-parts and repair networks
- Print/sign shops
- Independent ecommerce stores
- Property managers
- Campus communities
- Employee commute-benefit partnerships

## Viral loops

### Recipient loop
Every recipient sees tracking and a call to “Earn on your next trip.”

### Route loop
A runner saves a commute. The app automatically notifies them of matching requests; no repeated searching required.

### Business loop
Businesses invite customers and neighboring businesses. Credits unlock after real completed activity.

### Density loop
More routes shorten wait time; shorter wait time increases business volume; volume improves runner earnings; earnings recruit more routes.

## Anti-fake-growth controls

- No rewards for signups alone
- Referral reward after completed, non-refunded job
- Device/payment identity deduplication
- Rate limits
- Manual review of suspicious clusters
- Market-level incentive caps

## Million-car scoreboard

Track separately:

- Registered runner accounts
- Verified eligible runners
- Active route posters (30 days)
- Active runners completing jobs (30 days)
- Vehicles with current required documents

Never market “one million drivers” when the number is merely signups.
